is renamed from `erdos-585` to `erdos`; existing history and tags are retained. The contents of this

## Smaller-cutoff corollary

Let q ≥ 200704 be an integer. Every finite simple bipartite graph G on n ≥ 2q vertices with maximum
degree at most 2q − 2 and

$$
e(G)\ge(q-1)n-q^{3/2}/128
$$

contains a nonempty q-regular subgraph using original vertices and edges. The power and deficit are
interpreted over the reals. The conclusion does not require or promise inducedness, connectedness,
spanning support or the earlier larger-support guarantee.

The declarations in [R898/Corollary041.lean](../R898/Corollary041.lean) are
`Erdos585.R898.quantitative_numeric_200704` and
`Erdos585.R898.superlinear_forcing_200704`. The original `superlinear_forcing` endpoint at q ≥ 262144
and `every_h_forcing` parameterized theorem in `R898/Main.lean` are unchanged.

For x = √q and h = floor(x/64) − 1, the new cutoff gives x ≥ 448 and h ≥ 6. The existing estimates give
4096(h + 1)² ≤ q and

$$
hq-16h^2\ge q(x/64-513/256)\ge xq/128.
$$

This lowers the convenient corollary's cutoff by 61440, or 23.4375%. The existing parameterized
theorem already covered this degree range; no exponent improvement, optimal cutoff, new general
host class or unrestricted Erdős 585 bound is claimed.

## An existing avoiding-family example

At q = 9 the closed GeneralStar parameters are (s,p,h,b) = (2,4,4,16). The seed has 27 vertices and
160 edges, with deficit 8·27 − 160 = 56. The existing family endpoint supplies every n ≥ 27 with
8n − 56 edges, maximum degree at most 16 and no nonempty 9-regular subgraph. At common n ≥ 31,
this is two edges denser than the circular family's 8n − 58. This is an illustration of
[GeneralStar](../GeneralStar/Main.lean), not a new construction; its optimization is relative to the
specified parameter family.

## Reproduction and responsibility

From the repository root, the quick package-free demonstration is:

```bash
python3 -B graphs/verify_graphs.py
```

Use normal Python mode. `-O` and `PYTHONOPTIMIZE` optimization are rejected by the verifiers.
Full formal reproduction uses the pinned Lean toolchain and dependencies, `lake build`,
`scripts/check_axioms.py`, each registered whole-file `check.sh` call, and
`python3 -B scripts/replay_modules.py`. Each Lean replay has its own 240-second timeout.

The [verification record](supplement/checks/v0.4.1/verification.json) binds the checked source
closure, toolchain, dependency lockfile and checking tools, and lists actual check outcomes.
Historical v0.4 receipts retain their dates and meanings. No complete census regeneration or full
certificate-stream replay is claimed for this correction release.

AI agents produced the new corollary, tooling changes and documentation under Robert's direction.
The corollary received a separate fresh-context AI review. Robert remains responsible for the
release. These checks and reviews establish neither historical novelty nor human mathematical
endorsement. The existing detailed contribution record is in [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md).
