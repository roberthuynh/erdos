# Sources and comparison

Comparison refreshed 16 October 2026. This is a bounded public-source comparison, not a worldwide priority certificate. Full papers are cited, not redistributed.

| Source | Exact relevance and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Füredi, *Hypergraphs in which all disjoint pairs have distinct unions*, Combinatorica 4 (1984), 161–168, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02579216) | Original disjoint-union framework and lower constructions. The earlier campaign inspected selected statements and the design construction; this preparation did not reread the complete proof. |
| Pikhurko–Verstraëte, *The Maximum Size of Hypergraphs without Generalized 4-Cycles*, JCTA 116 (2009), 637–649, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2008.09.002), [author manuscript](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~jverstra/generalizedc4.pdf) | Theorem 2 gives the all-n 13/9 bound. Lemmas 3, 7 and 11 supply the attributed deletion/separation/cleaning inputs. The downloaded 18-page author manuscript is dated 30 December 2007; it is not the journal typeset version. These theorem and lemma clauses were rechecked in preparation; prior exact-source reviews inspected the proof dependencies. |
| Huang–Ma–Yang, *Extremal hypergraphs without generalized 4-cycles*, [arXiv:2609.37744v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37744v1), 29 September 2026 | The main result is for fixed r≥4 and sufficiently large n. Section 7 leaves r=3 open. The deletion framework motivated the pair-charge approach; no r≥4 theorem is specialized to r=3 in the formal proof. Introduction, main statement and closing discussion were compared; this is not a fresh review of every technical argument. |
| Marchant, *The Largest Minimum Codegree of a 3-Graph Without a Generalized 4-Cycle*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548312000430) | Minimum-codegree conditions concern a different parameter from total edge count. This preparation carries forward the earlier abstract-level distinction; it does not claim a new full-paper audit. |

The papers use a maximum avoiding count, whereas the [problem page](https://www.erdosproblems.com/643) uses a minimum forcing threshold. The latter adds one to the integer maximum. The claimed saving is against 13/9 in binomial normalization, not against 13/9 times n².

The live [discussion](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/643) and [proof-claim page](https://www.erdosproblems.com/forum/thread/643/proof-claims) were retrieved in preparation. The claims page displayed no submitted proof claims. That absence is not evidence of priority. Targeted title/author searches found the named primary sources; they do not exhaust citations, revisions or alternate formulations. The author-manuscript versus journal-version comparison for PV remains a limitation for bibliographic priority assessment.
