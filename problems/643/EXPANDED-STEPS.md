# Expanded proof steps

The expanded arguments are included in full in the appendix of the [written proof](SOLUTION.md) and in the [manuscript source](manuscript/sections/expanded-arguments.tex).

| Appendix subsection | Argument |
|---|---|
| A | Avoidance is equivalent to intersecting common links, without a linearity assumption. |
| B | Every actual host gives a feasible profile; the numerical domain may be larger. |
| C | All six relabellings needed for the bicone nondeficiency argument. |
| D | Exceptional-slot accounting, including the corrected expression and its monotonicity. |
| E | Donor shares and proportional recovery pay each deficit without double counting. |
| F | Analytic tails and the exhaustive partition of the profile domain. |

The main text and appendix use the same notation and lemma names. These written arguments do not establish full-theorem Lean verification or human mathematical acceptance. See the [verification record](VERIFICATION.md).
