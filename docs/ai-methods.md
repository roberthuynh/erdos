# AI-assisted methods

AI agents read eligible public literature, propose arguments, write formalizations and search programs, and review candidate work. [How this was made](../README.md#how-this-was-made) and [model contributions](../problems/585/findings/FINDINGS.md#7-ai-use-and-model-contributions) describe that work and its limits. The [#60 disclosure](../problems/60/README.md#ai-use) describes its written proofs, Lean formalization and AI review.

Separate-agent review is a useful error filter. Reviewers can share assumptions or repeat mistakes, including when they use the same model. Report reviewer type, independence and inspected scope. Do not describe AI review as human mathematical review.

Keep written claims, formal declarations, computational experiments and source comparisons distinguishable. Pin the statement and dependencies before checking them. Record exact commands and outcomes, use bounded pilots for expensive searches and preserve failed or incomplete checks as such. Independent implementations strengthen a computational check only to the extent that their inputs and methods are independent.

Credit prior mathematics and disclose source-reading limits. An unsuccessful search for an earlier theorem does not establish novelty. Confidential or held third-party material has no place in this public collection.
